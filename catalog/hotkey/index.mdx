`display.Hotkey` takes keys joined by `+`, such as `mod+shift+k`. `mod` is Command on Apple devices and Ctrl elsewhere; `Platform` picks the naming, or asks the device when left at `HotkeyAuto`.

## Variants

`Compact` puts every key in one cap, as tooltips show them. `Borderless` shows the keys as text.
